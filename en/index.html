Why this path is becoming necessary

More and more people are seeking not a new address, but a new form of life

Today in many countries the economy may keep working, technology may keep advancing and cities may keep shining. But the fabric of life itself is unravelling: families lose stability, children grow up without a cultural foundation, education detaches from reality, and land ceases to be home and becomes a commodity.

Against this background, Russia is increasingly seen as one of the few remaining spaces where it is still possible to build life on a different foundation: family, tradition, land, service, education and a long-term will.

Legal status, timelines and specific actions depend on country of origin, family situation, professional profile and the current legal framework. The route is built individually, within the overall pilot system.

Why territory matters more than bureaucracy

A person can be brought to a country. They must be brought into life

The main mistake of most immigration models is that they bring a person to a country, but not into life. They may process a visa, translate documents and even help with initial housing — but then the person is left alone with the language, school, bank, registration, daily environment and inner emptiness.

We start from a different principle: a person needs a territory to take root in, and the entry procedure is only part of it. That is why a receiving environment is at the centre of the route.

The psychological formula of the project
  • Not emigration, but rooting
  • Not a solitary move, but entry into a receiving environment
  • Not chaos, but a designed route
  • Not a promise of miracles, but serious work with reality

Here you are invited into a designed territory of life.

What your new life could become

A home on the land. Children in a living environment. Work connected to the future

For some this may be an agrarian laboratory and work with living systems. For others, an engineering or IT role within a territorial project. For others still, a school, a craft, a family enterprise, land restoration, a health environment or the architecture of a new settlement.

It is about entering a new assembly of life, where a person finds a name, a purpose and a horizon.

Russia Homestead in a broader context

Part of a broader civilisational project

Russia Homestead is the practical route. The Russia Charter sets out the values and the century-long horizon of 2026–2126: family, home, land, culture and continuity across generations. The Charter explains why; Russia Homestead shows how to get there in practice.

Applying to Russia Homestead does not require participation in, or a contribution to, the Charter, and a Charter participation level gives no advantage when an application is reviewed.
